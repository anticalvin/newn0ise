HOW IT WORKS

Write standard p5.js using setup() and draw(). Read values from params.speed, density, scale, hue, sat, bright and trail, plus any slider you add. lum(u,v) and px(u,v) read your loaded photo. Change sliders while your sketch runs. Save locally, remix, and export a still.

Ctrl / Cmd + Enter runs the sketch. The preview is isolated from the editor.
